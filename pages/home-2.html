Due diligence

Questions the C-suite asks.

How is this different from a traditional relocation agency?

Agencies sell vendor hours. Mobiliti Executive is a governed software workspace plus a named specialist per file — every stage requires evidence and sign-off, and leadership gets live portfolio reporting instead of monthly PDFs.

Who owns immigration risk?

Our in-house counsel network across 140+ countries. Filings, legalisations and dependent visas are tracked with counsel notes and decision documents stored in your encrypted vault for audit.

Can we keep our existing movers and housing agents?

Yes. Bring your preferred vendors into the portal with contracted SLAs, or use our pre-vetted network at negotiated enterprise rates. Either way you get one consolidated invoice.

How fast can we onboard?

Most enterprises run their first live relocation within two weeks: policy tiers configured, users provisioned with role-based access, and historical files migrated by our team.
