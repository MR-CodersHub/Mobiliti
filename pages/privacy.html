Data Security & Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we collect, protect and respect your data across every relocation.

Last updated: October 15, 2026 GDPR · SOC 2 aligned

1. Information We Collect

Mobiliti collects information to operate effectively and provide you with the best relocation experiences. We act as a Data Processor for corporate clients (Data Controllers) regarding employee data.

Types of Data Collected

  • Personal Identity Data: Names, dates of birth, passport information, and government-issued IDs necessary for visa processing.
  • Contact Data: Email addresses, phone numbers, and physical addresses.
  • Family Data: Details of accompanying dependents (spouses, children) required for immigration and schooling logistics.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with our dashboards, IP addresses, and browser types.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely for the purpose of fulfilling corporate mobility services and improving our platform.

Primary Uses

  • Processing work permits, visas, and legal residency documents.
  • Coordinating logistics such as freight forwarding, corporate housing, and flights.
  • Providing customer support via our in-platform chat and 24/7 hotline.
  • Generating aggregated, anonymized expense reports for HR departments.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Global mobility inherently requires sharing information with specialized local entities. We never sell your personal data to advertisers.

Who We Share Data With

  • Government Authorities: Embassies, consulates, and border agencies for legal compliance and visa approvals.
  • Vetted Service Partners: Local immigration attorneys, moving companies, and real estate agents. All partners are bound by strict data processing agreements (DPAs).
  • Corporate Employers: HR departments receive status updates and cost data related to your relocation.

4. Your Rights & GDPR Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Your Rights Include

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request deletion of data, subject to legal obligations for retaining immigration records.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of data to another organization.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to protect your sensitive personal and corporate data.

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
  • Access Controls: Strict role-based access control (RBAC) ensures only authorized personnel can view sensitive documents.
  • Retention: We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal retention requirements (e.g., tax and immigration laws).
